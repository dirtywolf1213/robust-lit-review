0 Key Findings

This site summarizes 40 randomized controlled trials (2015–2025) of GLP-1 and dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonists for weight loss in adults with obesity but without type 2 diabetes. Every trial is drawn from a Q1 journal (17 from the New England Journal of Medicine) and carries a validated DOI.

Bottom line: Incretin-based therapy is the most effective pharmacologic treatment for non-diabetic obesity to date. Semaglutide 2.4 mg (STEP program) and the dual agonist tirzepatide (SURMOUNT program) produce large, dose-dependent weight loss; in a direct head-to-head trial tirzepatide reached a least-squares mean weight change of −20.2% at 72 weeks .
