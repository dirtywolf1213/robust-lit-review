2 Semaglutide — the STEP program

The pivotal STEP 1 trial randomized 1961 adults with obesity (BMI ≥30, or ≥27 with a weight-related comorbidity) and without diabetes to once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg versus placebo over 68 weeks . Companion trials extended this to intensive behavioral therapy , to weight-loss maintenance after a run-in , and to two-year durability .

Earlier phase 2 dose-ranging work established the dose–response relationship motivating the 2.4 mg dose , and mechanistic studies showed reduced energy intake and appetite as the driver . Adolescent data (STEP TEENS) confirmed efficacy in younger populations .
