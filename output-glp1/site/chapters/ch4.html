4 Next-generation & oral agents

The pipeline is moving toward oral and combination therapies that may improve access and adherence:

  • Oral semaglutide 25 mg demonstrated efficacy in overweight/obesity .
  • Orforglipron, an oral small-molecule (non-peptide) GLP-1 agonist, reduced weight without injection or cold-chain burden .
  • CagriSema (cagrilintide–semaglutide) combines an amylin analogue with semaglutide for additive effect .
  • Liraglutide, the first-generation daily agonist (SCALE), remains a reference comparator .

Several trials extended endpoints beyond the scale to heart-failure symptoms and cardiometabolic risk in obesity .
