3 Tirzepatide 與頭對頭比較Tirzepatide & head-to-head

The dual GIP/GLP-1 agonist tirzepatide produced the largest weight reductions in this evidence base. SURMOUNT-1 established efficacy in non-diabetic obesity ; maintenance trials showed that continued therapy preserves weight loss while discontinuation precipitates regain . Further trials evaluated tirzepatide across populations and endpoints including cardiometabolic risk .

Direct comparison: In a phase 3b open-label trial (n = 751, 72 weeks) in adults with obesity but without type 2 diabetes, the least-squares mean percent change in body weight was −20.2% with tirzepatide, with secondary endpoints (≥15%, ≥20%, ≥25% weight reduction) favoring tirzepatide over semaglutide .

雙重 GIP/GLP-1 agonist tirzepatide 在本證據基礎中產生了最大幅度的減重。 SURMOUNT-1 確立其在 non-diabetic obesity 的療效 ；維持期試驗顯示持續用藥可保留減重成果， 而停藥則會導致體重回升 。其他試驗則在不同族群與終點 （含 cardiometabolic risk）評估 tirzepatide 。

直接比較：在一項 phase 3b open-label 試驗中（n = 751，72 週）， 針對有 obesity 但無 type 2 diabetes 的成人，tirzepatide 的 least-squares mean 體重百分比變化為 −20.2%；在次要終點（≥15%、≥20%、≥25% 減重比例）上， tirzepatide 亦優於 semaglutide 。
